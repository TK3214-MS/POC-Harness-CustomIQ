# Retail Terminology Glossary (Synthetic Demo Pack)

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Store | A synthetic retail store. |
| Product | A synthetic retail product. |
| InventoryRecord | On-hand inventory of a Product at a Store. |
| Order | A replenishment order for a Product at a Store. |
| DemandSignal | A detected demand change (spike/decline/seasonal) for a Product at a Store. |
| reorder_point | Synthetic threshold below which on-hand quantity triggers a reorder review. |

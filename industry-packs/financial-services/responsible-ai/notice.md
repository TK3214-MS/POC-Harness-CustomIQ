# Responsible AI Notice - Financial Services Industry Pack

This response is generated from **synthetic** Financial Services data in **Local Preview Mode**. It is a decision-support aid only.

- It never automatically freezes an account.
- It never automatically denies or reverses a transaction.
- It never automatically makes a legal determination about fraud.
- It never automatically scores an individual's creditworthiness.
- A qualified fraud analyst must review the case; a compliance officer must approve closure or any account-level action.
- All entities (customers, accounts, transactions, fraud cases) are fictional and generated for demonstration purposes only.

See also: `industry-packs/financial-services/manifest.yaml` (`prohibited_actions`, `human_approval_rules`) and the repository-wide [SECURITY.md](../../../SECURITY.md).

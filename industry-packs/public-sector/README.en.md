# industry-packs/public-sector/

[![日本語](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E3%81%82-%E6%97%A5%E6%9C%AC%E8%AA%9E-5B6670?style=for-the-badge)](README.md) [![English](https://img.shields.io/badge/A-English-087F8C?style=for-the-badge)](README.en.md)

This is the Industry Pack for the Public Sector. Its standard scenario is Public Sector Case Investigation.

Prohibited actions: automatically determining benefits or permits, automating administrative sanctions, ranking citizen risk, and making decisions based on protected attributes.

The implemented entities are SyntheticCitizen, Agency, Case, and Application. `manifest.yaml` is validated with `IndustryPackManifest`.

For usage instructions, see the [lab portal](../../docs/index.md) or the [production environment setup guide](../../docs/Production-Environment-Setup.md).

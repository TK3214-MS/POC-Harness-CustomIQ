This is the Industry Pack for Retail. Its standard scenario is Retail Demand and Inventory Analysis.

The implemented entities are Store, Product, InventoryRecord, Order, and DemandSignal. `manifest.yaml` is validated with `IndustryPackManifest`.

For usage instructions, see the [lab portal](../../docs/index.md) or the [production environment setup guide](../../docs/Production-Environment-Setup.md).

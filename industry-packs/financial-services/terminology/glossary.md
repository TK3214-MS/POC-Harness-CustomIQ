# Financial Services Terminology Glossary (Synthetic Demo Pack)

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Customer | A synthetic bank customer. |
| Account | An account owned by a Customer. |
| Transaction | A transaction posted against an Account. |
| FraudCase | A fraud investigation case opened against a flagged Transaction. |
| risk_score | Synthetic 0.0-1.0 score representing a FraudCase's estimated fraud likelihood. |
